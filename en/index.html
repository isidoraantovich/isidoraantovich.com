Dating

I dream of one day finding my prince charming

I’ve always loved the idea of meeting someone who could sweep me off my feet — so I didn’t build one profile, I built two versions of me. Fun me is a creative designer who’s just here for a good time; serious me is looking for something real (I still haven’t decided what serious me does for a living — give me time). Fun me vapes and keeps it casual. Serious, wholesome, non-smoking me somehow ticked “open to relocate,” while fun me put “not sure” — which is, of course, entirely backwards. And just to keep things interesting, I gave us a little variation right down to the ideal first date: fun me wants a quiet coffee, serious me wants a “friend / activity partner.” We’re both good Christian girls, too, of course — it would be a sin to mislead anyone. Am I scamming the guys I go out with? Cheeky. Who knows. ♡

Good-time me is my favourite. She’s been around since March 2025, and she’s still the one I keep coming back to. Serious me is new, like May 2026 new and not a whole lot of fun but she gets the job done. Either way, I think they both speak for themselves, don’t you?
